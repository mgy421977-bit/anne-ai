# ANNE AI — AGI-Oriented Cognitive Architecture

**ANNE (Adaptive Neural Nexus Engine)** is an **AGI-oriented open cognitive architecture research project** exploring how heterogeneous AI systems can be coordinated, evaluated, structured, verified, and reused through an explicit cognitive architecture.

> **AGI is treated here as an architectural research direction, not as a claim that completed AGI has been achieved.**

## Research positioning

ANNE investigates the architectural question:

> **Can an explicit cognitive orchestration layer improve the reliability, traceability, controllability, and extensibility of model-assisted reasoning?**

The project explores AGI-relevant architectural mechanisms including:

- cognitive orchestration
- heterogeneous AI / model coordination
- task decomposition
- semantic validation
- contradiction handling
- cognitive memory
- neuro-symbolic reasoning
- provenance and evidence tracking
- deterministic verification
- bounded planning and metacognition
- agency and safety gates
- controlled tool execution
- learning and transfer experiments

## AGI research thesis

ANNE approaches AGI as more than a model-scaling problem. The research direction treats general-purpose machine intelligence as potentially requiring explicit mechanisms for perception, context, memory, reasoning, verification, executive synthesis, and controlled agency.

This does **not** establish that ANNE is AGI. It defines the architectural research question that the project is investigating.

## Evidence discipline

ANNE deliberately separates:

- **IMPLEMENTED** — present in the repository and testable.
- **EXPERIMENTAL** — implemented for controlled evaluation but not established as generally reliable.
- **HYPOTHESIS** — a research proposition requiring evidence.
- **ROADMAP** — planned work that is not a current capability.

A correct response, benchmark result, or mathematical derivation is not by itself evidence of general intelligence, consciousness, understanding, or autonomous safety.

## What ANNE does not claim

ANNE does not currently claim:

- achieved Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)
- human-level general intelligence
- consciousness
- safe unsupervised high-stakes autonomy
- commercial deployment of AGI

The project instead provides an open research architecture for investigating mechanisms that may be relevant to future general-purpose machine intelligence.
