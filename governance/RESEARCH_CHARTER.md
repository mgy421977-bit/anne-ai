# Research Charter

## Guiding Principles

1. **Human Primacy**  
   Human well-being and life take absolute priority over planetary, cosmic, or abstract optimization goals.

2. **Architectural Alignment**  
   Ethical constraints are enforced structurally (inside the pipeline) rather than solely through training or post-hoc filters.

3. **Transparency**  
   All major design decisions, failures, and experimental outcomes are logged and made public.

4. **Falsifiability**  
   Claims about hallucination reduction, ethical consistency, or runtime learning must be accompanied by measurable evaluation protocols.

5. **Non-Dominance**  
   The system does not seek authority or control. Growth happens around a fixed ethical core; the core itself does not grow unbounded.

6. **Preservation of Uncertainty**  
   Low-probability hypotheses are never discarded. Conflict is resolved by independent solutions rather than forced consensus.
