# ANNE as an AGI Candidate Architecture

**Status:** research positioning document  
**Claim level:** architectural research direction — **not** a claim that AGI has been achieved.

## One-sentence stance

ANNE is an experimental cognitive architecture and **AGI candidate concept** exploring a path toward **general-purpose intelligence**.

ANNE is **not** presented as a completed AGI system, and it does **not** claim human-level general intelligence.

## Research question

> Can general-purpose intelligence emerge from—or be approached by—a cognitive architecture built around hypothesis generation, evidence boundaries, verification, memory, metacognition, agency constraints, abstention, and bounded iterative reasoning?

This is an **empirical and architectural** question. It is not answered by this repository alone.

## Why “candidate architecture” (not “AGI”)

| Phrase | Allowed? | Meaning |
|--------|----------|---------|
| AGI candidate concept | Yes | A design worth testing on the path to general-purpose intelligence |
| AGI research direction | Yes | Mechanisms studied are AGI-relevant |
| Completed AGI | **No** | Unsupported |
| Human-level intelligence | **No** | Unsupported |
| Energy AGI / domain AGI product | **No** | Mis-positioning |

## Mechanism families under study

- Cognitive architectures and orchestration
- Hypothesis / candidate generation (MITOS-class proposals)
- Evidence-aware reasoning and verification boundaries
- Metacognition and bounded retry
- Persistent cognitive memory and failure traces
- AI abstention (refuse or halt when unsupported)
- Agency boundaries and human–AI decision interfaces
- Model-agnostic executive layers (providers optional)

## Relationship to applications

ANNE is **domain-general** as a research object. Industrial energy planning, BOM, or commercial pricing are **applications** that may sit above or beside ANNE (for example via VITA). They are not the definition of ANNE.
