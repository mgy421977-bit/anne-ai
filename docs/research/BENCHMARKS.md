# Benchmarks (research)

## Intent

Benchmarks measure the **cognitive loop**, not marketing scores.

Example scenario family (from architecture demos): industrial GES / BESS / efficiency comparison questions.

**Goal of the scenario:** exercise DUY→…→YAP/ABSTAIN and evidence boundaries—not to publish a verified commercial energy design from ANNE alone.

## Suggested metrics

- Task success under equal definitions (Raw LLM vs RAG vs ANNE path)
- False-accept / false-reject under EvidenceGate
- Inference calls and tokens per completed task
- Abstention rate on under-specified factual asks
- Failure-trace usefulness on retry

## Honesty

No leaderboard superiority is claimed in this document. Feature-branch experiments are not `main` capabilities until merged and CI-verified.
